Our Mission

Sakura was created to give every learner a free, effective, and beautiful way to study Japanese. Whether you're preparing for the JLPT exam, applying for an SSW visa, or studying business Japanese for BJT — Sakura provides structured courses, flashcards, quizzes, and kanji practice all in one place.

We believe language learning should be accessible to all, regardless of financial background. That's why Sakura is and will always remain free.
