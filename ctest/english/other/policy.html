Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

🌸 Your privacy is our priority at Sakura Academy

Welcome to Sakura Academy. We believe learning Japanese should be safe, private, and enjoyable. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it. By using our platform, you agree to these terms.

📧 Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our service:

  • Email address — used only for login and account security
  • Display name — shown on your profile and leaderboard
  • Profile photo — optional, uploaded by you
  • Learning progress — quiz scores, lessons completed, time studied
  • Community chat messages — automatically deleted within 24 hours
🔐 Your email address is stored in encrypted form and is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.

🛡️ How We Protect Your Email

Your email address is one of the most sensitive pieces of information you share with us. Here's how we protect it:

  • Stored using industry-standard encryption (AES-256)
  • Never sold or shared with advertisers, partners, or third parties
  • Used only for account login, password reset, and security alerts
  • You can request deletion of your email data at any time
  • Our servers use HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data transmission

💬 Community Chat Policy

Our community chat is designed for safe, supportive learning. Important rules:

  • All chat messages are automatically deleted within 24 hours
  • We do not store chat history permanently on our servers
  • You are responsible for what you post in public community spaces
  • Moderators may remove content that violates our community guidelines
⏱️ Chat messages disappear automatically after 24 hours — so your conversations stay private.

©️ Copyright & Content Protection

All content on Sakura Academy — including vocabulary lists, grammar explanations, kanji data, quiz questions, audio recordings, and page designs — is protected by copyright.

  • You may not copy, reproduce, or redistribute any content from this site without written permission
  • You may not scrape, download, or extract our content for use on other platforms
  • You may not use our content for commercial purposes or resale
  • Screenshots for personal study notes are permitted
  • Sharing links to our site is always welcome 🌸
⚠️ Unauthorized copying or redistribution of Sakura Academy content may result in account termination and legal action.

🤝 Community Conduct Rules

Sakura Academy is a welcoming space for learners of all backgrounds. In our community chat and forums, the following are strictly prohibited:

  • Hate speech, discrimination, or harassment targeting any individual or group
  • Offensive, obscene, or sexually explicit content
  • Bullying, threats, or intimidation of any community member
  • Spam, advertising, or promotion of external services
  • Sharing personal information of others without consent
  • Impersonating other users, moderators, or Sakura staff
  • Content promoting violence, self-harm, or illegal activities
🚫 Violations will result in immediate account suspension. Serious violations will be reported to relevant authorities.

🍪 Cookies & Analytics

We use minimal, privacy-respecting tools to improve your experience:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — anonymous usage statistics to improve the site
  • Firebase — secure authentication and progress tracking
  • localStorage — saves your theme preference and recent pages locally on your device
  • We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking
  • We do not sell your data to any third party

✅ Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction — update your name, email, or profile photo anytime
  • Deletion — request complete deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability — export your learning progress data
  • Opt-out — disable analytics tracking by using browser privacy settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: privacy@learnwithsakura.com

👶 Children's Privacy

Sakura Academy is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us immediately and we will delete the account and all associated data.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Sakura Academy after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We recommend reviewing this page periodically.
